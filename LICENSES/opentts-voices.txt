The preset voices in mova_tts/data/voices (tetiana, lada, mykyta, oleksa) are NeuCodec
encodings of one utterance each from the Ukrainian OpenTTS datasets:

  https://huggingface.co/datasets/speech-uk/opentts-tetiana
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/speech-uk/opentts-lada
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/speech-uk/opentts-mykyta
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/speech-uk/opentts-oleksa

Those datasets are released under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (see ../LICENSE).
The clip ids and transcripts are listed in mova_tts/data/voices/presets.json.
